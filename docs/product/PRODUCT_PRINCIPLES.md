# Product Principles

## 1. Trust before intelligence
Financial output must be explainable and traceable.

## 2. Evidence before confidence
A value without a reliable source must not be presented as verified.

## 3. Simple surface, deep engine
Users should not need to understand OCR, RAG, embeddings, agents, queues or schemas.

## 4. No fake authority
Do not claim CA review, official verification, certification, encryption level or regulatory status unless that capability actually exists and is verified.

## 5. Deterministic where possible
Arithmetic and rule-based financial calculations should use deterministic code.

## 6. Progressive disclosure
Show the simple answer first; expose calculation/source/details on demand.

## 7. User ownership and isolation
A user's financial data belongs to that user's authenticated tenant boundary.

## 8. No silent correction
If extracted data conflicts or cannot be verified, surface the problem.

## 9. No fabricated data
Demo data must be explicitly marked as demo. Production screens must not silently fall back to fake numbers.

## 10. AI is a reasoning/interface layer, not the financial source of truth
AI can classify, retrieve, explain and orchestrate. It must not override validated financial records or deterministic calculations.
